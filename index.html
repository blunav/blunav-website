Three layers of airport IT

Three suites, one foundation.

Airport IT stacks in three layers: the operational record, the operations and maintenance work that runs against it, and the airside picture. Most airports buy each from a different vendor. Aerios builds all three on one database, so nothing is re-keyed and every record shares one audit trail.

01Core platform

Aerios AMS

Schedules to screens. The airport's authoritative operational record.
  • Airport Operational Database (AODB)
  • Resource Management System (RMS)
  • Flight Information Display System (FIDS)

Seasonal SSIM planning, active flight and turnaround management, rule-based allocation of gates, stands, counters and belts on a rolling Gantt with conflict detection, and display management for every screen in the terminal plus APIs and an embeddable web widget.

02Ops & maintenance

Aerios Orbis

Digitised operations, maintenance and passenger service workflows.
  • Asset management (CMMS)
  • Inspections
  • Operations log
  • Lost & Found
  • Permit management
  • Badge management

Asset registry and lifecycle records, preventive maintenance, configurable inspection checklists with mobile forms, shift and occurrence logging with duty handover, and lost property with chain of custody. Permits and badges use the same stakeholder registry AMS already holds.

03Airside safety

Aerios Vecta

Airside vehicle tracking and safety. One live picture of the airfield.
  • Aircraft and vehicle tracking
  • Safety alerts and event detection
  • Geofencing and custom rules
  • Blipper tracking hardware
  • CCTV, NOTAM and METAR in the same view
  • 30-day airside replay

Aircraft via ADS-B and surveillance feeds, vehicles via GNSS, on one airport map. Incursion, speed, dwell-time and boundary alerts with configurable severity, and in-cab warnings from the Blipper retrofit unit at one-second updates and up to 1.5 m accuracy.

Vertically integrated

Every module sits on the same record.

A permit issued in Orbis references the airline and ground handler already registered in the AODB. An inspection defect raised on the apron becomes a work order without leaving the platform. A stand blocked for maintenance disappears from RMS allocation at the same moment.

That is what we mean by vertically integrated: one identity model, one mobile client, one automation engine and one audit trail, shared rather than duplicated across layers.

How we work

Your ops team's software partner, not their vendor.

01

We start small, on purpose.

Most airports begin with a single module and a single terminal. That first deployment tells both of us whether the fit is right. There's no minimum bundle.

02

Your people shape the roadmap.

When your AOCC or airside team tells us a workflow is wrong, that goes on the board, not into a backlog nobody reads. The modules we build next are the ones airports ask for.

03

You get a named engineer, not a ticket queue.

Each airport we work with has one person on our side who knows its layout, its subsystems and its people. That person answers the phone during operations.

04

Your data is yours.

Per-airport data segregation, and your records are exportable at any time. If you leave, you take everything with you in standard formats.

05

Built to international regulations. Strict on data privacy and residency.

Aerios follows ICAO and IATA operating standards and the DGCA and CERT-In requirements Indian airports answer to. Your data is segregated per airport, encrypted at rest and in transit, and hosted in the jurisdiction you choose so residency rules are met by design.
