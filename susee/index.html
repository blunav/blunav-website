Suseendar Marimuthu

Blunav System Private Ltd · Chennai

Suseendar Marimuthu is the Chief Executive Officer of Blunav, a visionary leader with deep expertise in aviation and airport operations. A product-focused strategist, he combines technical acumen with a profound passion for modernizing and optimizing the airport ecosystem. Under his leadership, Blunav is dedicated to developing innovative solutions that address the complex operational and navigational challenges faced by the aviation industry today.

With a distinguished background that includes international recognition—such as his selection for the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA) Space4Youth initiative—Suseendar brings a forward-thinking, multidisciplinary perspective to aerospace and aviation. His career is defined by a commitment to driving impactful technological advancements, empowering cross-functional teams, and shaping the future of smarter, more efficient global transit.
